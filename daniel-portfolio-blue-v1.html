01 - About

Commercial production
with systems thinking

Daniel Li / 李绵泰 is a UK based freelancer with a Bachelor's degree from Durham University and a Master's degree from the University of Warwick. His work spans commercial video, exhibition coverage, sports media, livestream direction and post-production.

He guided Durham University's student media PalTV to the 2024 NASTA top honour, Broadcaster of the Year, and has worked with teams and clients including Pico Group, Mindray Animal Medical, Yonex, Durham University, University of Warwick, BUKC and BUCS.

The difference is operational: Daniel combines camera craft, project management, AI video workflows, ComfyUI experimentation, Notion systems and UK property media context into one delivery stack.
